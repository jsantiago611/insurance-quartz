---
title: Producer
---
A Producer is a catch-all term for anyone who sells insurance—both [[Agent]]s and brokers.

It's a sales and marketing term. It "produces" business for the insurance company. It emphasizes the function (selling policies) over the specific legal role (agent vs. broker). If you're not sure whether someone is technically an agent for one company or an independent broker, you can just call them a producer. It's the safe, generic term for the person who helps you get into [[The Pool]].
