---
title: Exposure Unit
---
An Exposure Unit is the standardized measurement used for calculating insurance [[Premium]]. It's the "per" in the price.

This is how insurers make different risks comparable. How do you price the risk of a skyscraper versus a food truck? You break it down into units. So the rate might be $X per $1,000 of payroll for workers' comp, or $Y per $100 of property value, or $Z per car. The premium is then the [[Rate]] multiplied by the number of exposure units. It's the fundamental building block of pricing. Without this, underwriting would just be wild guessing.
