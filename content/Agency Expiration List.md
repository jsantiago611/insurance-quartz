---
title: Agency Expiration List
---

An [[Agency Expiration List]] is the customer list—the names, policies, and renewal dates of all the clients an [[Agent]] or agency services.

This seems like a simple piece of data, but it is the single most valuable asset in the insurance distribution business. It's the route to the revenue.

Who owns this list determines the balance of power between the agent and the insurer. In an [[Independent Agency]] system, the agent owns it. If they leave, they take their book of business with them. In an [[Exclusive Agency]] system, the insurer owns it. The agent is just a temporary manager of the relationship. This is the core economic conflict in insurance marketing.
