---
title: Claims Representative
---
A Claims Representative is the employee of the insurance company who investigates a claim and decides whether and how much to pay from [[The Pool]].

Look, this is a tough job. They are the gatekeepers of the money. Everyone who files a claim has a story, and the Claims Rep's job is to figure out if the story is true, if it's covered by the policy, and what the actual dollar value of the loss is. They are, depending on your perspective, either the hero who saves you from financial ruin or the villain who nickel-and-dimes you. They have to be part detective, part accountant, and part therapist. They are the human manifestation of The Pool's promise—and its limits.
