---
title: Underwriting Guidelines
---
[[Underwriting Guidelines]] are the rules and criteria an insurer gives its [[Underwriter]]s to follow when evaluating and accepting risks.

These are the rules of the game. They tell the underwriter what types of business to write, what to avoid, what price to charge, and what policy forms to use. They are the company's defense against both [[Adverse Selection]] and its own underwriters' bad judgment. They create consistency. Without guidelines, every underwriter would be making up their own rules, and The Pool would be a chaotic mess. They are the standardized playbook for deciding who gets into The Pool and on what terms.
