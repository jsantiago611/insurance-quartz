---
title: Life-Health Insurance
---
Life-Health Insurance is insurance that covers risks related to human life and health—dying, getting sick, living too long.

This is a different world from [[Property-Casualty Insurance]]. The timelines are long. The policies can last for decades or a lifetime. The claims are more certain—everyone dies eventually. So the [[Float]] is enormous and long-duration, which makes [[Investment Income]] absolutely paramount. The risks are also less about sudden accidents and more about mortality and morbidity tables. It's less about "if" and more about "when." It's a different kind of Pool, one that's built for a much slower, more predictable drip of payouts.
