---
title: Life-Health Insurance
---
[[Life-Health Insurance]] is insurance that covers risks related to human life and health—dying, getting sick, or living too long (which is a risk to your retirement savings).

This is a different universe from [[Property-Casualty Insurance]]. The timelines are long—policies can last a lifetime. The claims are more certain (everyone dies eventually). So the [[Float]] is enormous and long-duration, which makes [[Investment Income]] the absolute lifeblood of the business. The underwriting is less about "if" and more about "when." It's a different kind of Pool, one that's built for a slow, predictable drip of payouts over decades, not the sudden, violent splashes of a car crash or a fire.
