---
title: Distribution Channel
---

A [[Distribution Channel]] is how an insurance company sells its policies. It's the route a product takes to get from [[The Pool]] to you.

And you might think, "Who cares? It's just sales." But the method of distribution shapes the entire business. Is it through [[Agent]]s who are [[Exclusive Agency Marketing System|exclusive]] to one company? Is it through [[Direct Writer Marketing System|direct writers]] who are employees? Is it through independent brokers who shop around? The choice of channel determines who owns the customer relationship, what the [[Expense Ratio]] looks like, and how the underwriting gets done. It's the plumbing of the insurance world. It's not sexy, but if it gets clogged, nothing else works.
