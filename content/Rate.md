---
title: Rate
---
The [[Rate]] is the price per unit of insurance. It's the cost for each [[Exposure Unit]].

This is the fundamental building block of the [[Premium]]. The rate is the number you look up in the [[Rate Manual]]. It might be $0.50 per $100 of property value, or $1.20 per $1,000 of payroll. The premium is then (Rate) x (Number of Exposure Units). So if you have a $200,000 building and the rate is $0.50 per $100, you have 2,000 exposure units, and your premium is $1,000. It's how The Pool puts a price tag on a piece of risk.
