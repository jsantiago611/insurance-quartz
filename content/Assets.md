---
title: Assets
---

[[Assets]] are everything an insurance company owns—bonds, stocks, real estate, cash. It's the total value of [[The Pool]] itself.

This seems simple, but it's the foundation of everything. The [[Premium]] you pay go into The Pool (the assets). The claims paid out come from The Pool (the assets). The whole game is about making sure that the assets are always greater than the liabilities—the future claims they've promised to pay. This is [[Solvency]]. If the assets aren't enough, The Pool is empty, and everyone is just holding a worthless promise. This is why insurance regulators are so obsessed with what kinds of assets insurers can own. They need to be safe, liquid... boring. The Pool can't be invested in crypto. Probably.
