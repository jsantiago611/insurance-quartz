---
title: Assets
---

[[Assets]] are what the insurance company owns—mostly the bonds and stocks it bought with your [[Premium|premiums]]. This is the "float"—the money they get to invest between when you pay your premium and when they (maybe) pay a claim. The goal is to have more assets than [[Liabilities]], with the difference being [[Policyholders Surplus]], which is just the cushion that keeps regulators from shutting you down.
