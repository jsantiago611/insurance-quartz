--
title: Damages
---
Damage] is the money one person is legally obligated to pay to another because they caused them a loss.

This is the fundamental output of the liability system. You did a bad thing (or were negligent), a court says you owe money, that money is "damages." This is the entire reason you buy liability insurance. You're not buying insurance against "being sued"; you're buying insurance against having to pay damages. [[The Pool]] is there to cover that legal debt for you. It turns an unpredictable, potentially bankrupting legal obligation into a predictable premium.
