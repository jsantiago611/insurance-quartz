---
title: Independent Exposures
---

Independent Exposures are two or more loss exposures where the occurrence of a loss from one does not affect the probability of a loss from the other.

This is the statistical dream for [[The Pool]]. If all the risks are independent, then the law of large numbers works perfectly. One house burns down, another gets hail damage, a car gets in a fender bender—all uncorrelated events. The Pool can smoothly pay out these random, unrelated claims. The nightmare is the opposite: correlated risk. A hurricane, an earthquake, a pandemic. When exposures aren't independent, The Pool can get wiped out all at once. The entire practice of [[Reinsurance]] and [[Catastrophe Model]]ing is about dealing with the fact that exposures aren't always independent.
