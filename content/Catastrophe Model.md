---
title: Catastrophe Model
---
A [[Catastrophe Model]] is a fancy computer simulation that tries to predict the probability and cost of a major disaster. It's what [[Underwriter]]s use to price the risk of, like, a hurricane wiping out an entire coastline.

And you might think, "This is just sophisticated guessing." And you're not entirely wrong? I mean, it's a model. It's built on historical data and a bunch of assumptions about the future, which is inherently unknowable. It's a very expensive crystal ball. But it's the best tool The Pool has to avoid the "everyone drowns at once" scenario, which is the existential threat to the whole concept. So they run these simulations to figure out if they should even offer insurance on a Florida beach house, and if so, for how many millions of dollars.
