---
title: Adverse Selection
---
> [!tip] In one sentence...
> Adverse Selection is the problem where the people who are most likely to need money from [[The Pool]] are the ones most likely to try to get into it. 

And is this is pretty much the core problem of insurance? 

The entire business is a bet. You're betting you'll have a loss, the insurer is betting you won't. But it you *know* you're a terrible risk - you're a rock climber with a need for speed - you're going to be super eager to buy life insurance for sedentary-office-worker prices. The pool would fill up with rock climbers and sink! The entire process of [[Underwriting]] is basically just a defense against adverse selection. It's the process of figuring out who's a rock climber and charging them extra, or just showing them the door.
