---
title: Adverse Selection
---


[[Adverse Selection]] is what happens when the free-solo rock climber buys life insurance at the "office worker" rate. It's the fundamental problem of insurance: the people who most want to buy it are the ones you least want to sell it to. The entire job of the [[Underwriter]] is to fight this—to make sure the rock climbers get charged rock climber prices so the [[The Pool|Pool]] doesn't end up full of people who are statistically likely to, you know, not be around for long.
