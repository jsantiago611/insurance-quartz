---
title: Binder
---

A [[Binder]] is a temporary, legally binding agreement that proves you're covered right now, even before the full policy is written. It's a promise from [[The Pool]].

And you're like, "This seems sketchy, right? It's not even the real contract." But look, business has to happen. You need to drive the car off the lot today. The bank needs proof of insurance for the closing this afternoon. The world doesn't stop because the underwriter's printer is slow. So the [[Agent]], who has the authority to speak for The Pool, can issue this temporary "you're in" slip. It's the insurance equivalent of a handshake deal, but, you know, with legal force. It's a bridge between the verbal "yes" and the 50-page pdf.
