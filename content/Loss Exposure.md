---
title: Loss Exposure
---
A [[Loss Exposure]] is any condition or situation that presents the possibility of a loss. It's a thing that could cause you to need money from [[The Pool]].

This is the raw material of insurance. Before you can insure something, you have to identify the exposure. Do you own a building? That's a property loss exposure. Do you drive a car? That's a liability and physical damage loss exposure. Do you have employees? That's a workers' comp loss exposure. The process of [[Risk Management]] starts with spotting all your loss exposures. You can't get into The Pool until you can point to what might go wrong.
