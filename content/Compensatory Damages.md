---
title: Compensatory Damages
---
[[Compensatory Damages]] are money awarded to a plaintiff to compensate them for a actual, measurable loss. It's the "making whole" part of a liability claim.

This is the core of [[Indemnify|indemnity]] in the legal system. If someone sues you and wins, they don't get to win the lottery; they get paid back for what they lost. Medical bills, lost wages, the cost to repair their car. The goal is to put them back in the position they were in before your negligence messed things up. It's the reason [[The Pool]] exists for liability—to pay these damages on your behalf. It's not a bonus; it's a reimbursement.
