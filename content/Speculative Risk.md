---
title:  Speculative Risk
---
A [[Speculative Risk]] is a situation where there is a chance of loss, but also a chance of gain. Like investing in the stock market or starting a business.

And this is the crucial distinction: insurance is not for speculative risk. Insurance is for [[Pure Risk]]—situations where there is only a chance of loss, or no loss. Your house burns down (loss) or it doesn't (no loss). You can't buy insurance against your stock portfolio going down, because that's a speculative risk—it could also go up. The Pool is designed to handle pure risks. If you let in speculative risks, the whole system breaks down because people would be betting on both sides. It would be a casino, not insurance.
