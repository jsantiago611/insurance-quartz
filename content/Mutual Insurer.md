---
title: Mutual Insurer
---
A Mutual Insurer is an insurance company that is owned by its policyholders. It's a co-op version of [[The Pool]].

In this model, the people who are in The Pool also own The Pool. Any profits that are left over after claims and expenses—the [[Policyholders Surplus]] that grows—can be paid back to the policyholders in the form of dividends. Or it can be retained to strengthen the company. The incentive is slightly different from a [[Stock Insurer]]; the goal is to provide insurance at the lowest possible net cost to the member-policyholders, rather than to generate a profit for outside shareholders. It's insurance for us, by us.
