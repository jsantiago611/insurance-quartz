---
title: Agency Expiration List
---

The [[Agency Expiration List]] is the customer list. It's the most valuable thing an insurance agency owns—the list of who their customers are and when their policies renew. This is why there's a power struggle: [[Independent Agency|independent agents]] own their list and can shop your business around, while [[Exclusive Agency Marketing System|exclusive agents]] don't—the insurance company does. Whoever owns the list owns the relationship.
