---
title: Predictive Modeling
---
Predictive Modeling is the use of statistical techniques and algorithms to forecast future outcomes, like the likelihood of a claim.

This is the modern, fancy version of underwriting. Instead of just looking at a few basic factors, you feed a thousand data points into a model—credit score, buying habits, satellite imagery of your roof—and it spits out a probability. It's [[Catastrophe Model]]ing, but for individual risks. It's more data-driven, but it also raises all sorts of questions about fairness and discrimination. Is it okay to charge someone more because their zip code has a higher claims history? The model doesn't care about "fair"; it just cares about correlation. It's a powerful tool for fighting [[Adverse Selection]], but it's also a bit creepy.
