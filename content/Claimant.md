---
title: Claimant
---
A Claimant is anyone who asks for money from [[The Pool]].

This seems straightforward, right? It's the person who got hurt and wants to be paid. But it's not always the [[Insured]]. If you crash into someone else's car, they are the claimant against your insurance. They're standing at the edge of The Pool with their hand out, saying, "His splash got me wet, and I need to be dried off." The Pool has to figure out if that's true, and if so, with how many towels.
