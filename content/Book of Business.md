---
title: Book of Business
---

A [[Book of Business]] is the total collection of insurance policies that an [[Agent]], agency, or insurer has written.

For an [[Underwriter]], it's their portfolio of risks. They don't just look at one policy; they manage a whole book, trying to make sure it's balanced and profitable (The [[Underwriter]]' entire job is to make this book "growing and profitable," which is corporate-speak for "get more people to pay us money without too many of them asking for it back."). For an [[Agent]], it's their livelihood—their recurring commissions and their source of renewals.

You'll hear phrases like "a profitable book of business" or "a book heavy in coastal property." It's the aggregate of all the individual bets, and managing it well is the difference between success and failure.
