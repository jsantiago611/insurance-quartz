---
title: Surplus Lines Insurer
---
A [[Surplus Lines Insurer]] is a specialty insurer that provides coverage for unique, high-risk, or hard-to-place exposures that standard insurers won't touch.

This is the "island of misfit risks." If your risk is too weird, too big, or too scary for the standard market, you go to the surplus lines market. These insurers are less heavily regulated in the specific rates and forms they can use, which gives them the flexibility to price and design coverage for bizarre situations. Think of a movie star's legs, a rocket launch, or a nightclub with a history of fights. They are a vital, niche part of the ecosystem, running a very specialized, high-stakes Pool.
