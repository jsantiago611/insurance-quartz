---
title: Stock Insurer
---
A Stock Insurer is an insurance company that is owned by its shareholders, like any other public corporation.

The goal here is to generate a profit for those shareholders. This is different from a [[Mutual Insurer]], which is owned by its policyholders. Both types run [[The Pool]], but the stock insurer's primary duty is to its outside investors. Profits can be paid out as dividends to shareholders or retained as [[Policyholders Surplus]]. The trade-off is that the focus might be more on growth and profitability than on providing insurance at the lowest possible net cost.
