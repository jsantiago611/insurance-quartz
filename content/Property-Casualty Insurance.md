---
title: Property-Casualty Insurance
---
Property-Casualty Insurance is insurance that covers damage to property and legal liability for injuries to others. It's everything except life and health.

This is the world of stuff and lawsuits. Your car, your house, your business, your potential legal liability to other people. The policies are generally short-term (one year). The claims are uncertain—maybe your house burns down, maybe it doesn't. The [[Float]] is shorter-term than in life insurance, and [[Investment Income]] is less dominant. The underwriting profit (or loss) as measured by the [[Combined Ratio]] is a much more immediate concern. This is the classic, volatile, "betting against disaster" version of [[The Pool]].
