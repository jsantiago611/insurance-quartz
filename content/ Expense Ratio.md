---
title:  Expense Ratio
---

The [[Expense Ratio]] is the percentage of each premium dollar that goes toward running the insurance company itself. Salaries, rent, marketing, and most of all, [[Agent]] commissions.

This is one of the two halves of the [[Combined Ratio]], and it's a measure of efficiency. A low expense ratio means the insurer is lean; it doesn't cost them much to collect a dollar of premium. A high expense ratio means they're spending a lot on overhead. If the [[Loss Ratio]] is the cost of the product itself, the expense ratio is the cost of the store. And if you add them together and get over 100%, the insurer is technically losing money on its underwriting... which is fine, I guess, if they can make it up on [[Investment Income]]? It's a weird business.
