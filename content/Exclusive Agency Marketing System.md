---
title: Exclusive Agency Marketing System
---
In an [[Exclusive Agency Marketing System]], the [[Agent]] sells insurance for only one company, but they are an independent contractor, not an employee.

The key here—the really important bit—is who owns the customer list. In this system, the insurance company owns the [[Agency Expiration List]]. So if the agent quits or gets fired, they can't take their book of business with them to a competitor. The customers stay with the company. It's like a franchise model. The agent runs the local storefront for The Pool, but the Pool owns the brand and the customer database. The agent is independent in how they run their office, but they are exclusive in what they sell. It's a hybrid. It's a way for the insurer to have a local presence without the hassle of actually employing all the salespeople.
