---
title: Principal
---

In an agency relationship, the [[Principal]] is the one who authorizes an [[Agent]] to act on their behalf. In insurance, the insurance company is the principal.

The agent has the power to bind the principal. When the agent says "you're covered," the company is on the hook. This is a classic legal relationship. The principal grants authority, and is then responsible for the actions of the agent within that authority. So when you buy a policy from a State Farm agent, you're not really buying it from the agent; you're buying it from State Farm, the principal. The agent is just the conduit to [[The Pool]].
