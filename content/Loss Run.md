---
title: Loss Run
---
A Loss Run is a report from an insurance company that details the history of claims for a particular policy or insured.

This is the report card. It's the history of your relationship with [[The Pool]]. When you go to get a new insurance quote, the new insurer will ask for your loss runs from your old insurers. They want to see your claims history. It's the data that tells them if you're a good bet or a money pit. It's the tangible evidence of your risk. It's your transcript, and it follows you forever.
