--- 
title: Risk Management
---
[[Risk Management]] is the process of identifying, evaluating, and dealing with potential losses. Insurance is just one tool in the risk management toolbox.

The process is: 1) Find your [[Loss Exposure]]s. 2) Analyze them. 3) Decide what to do. You can avoid the risk (don't do the risky thing). You can control it (install sprinklers). You can retain it (self-insure with a [[Deductible]]). Or you can transfer it (buy insurance, which is paying [[The Pool]] to take it off your hands). Thinking of insurance as just one option in risk management is key. 

The goal isn't to insure everything; the goal is to handle risk in the most efficient way possible.
