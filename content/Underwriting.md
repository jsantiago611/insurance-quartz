---
title: Underwriting
---
[[Underwriting]] is the process of selecting risks for [[The Pool]] and deciding on the terms and price of coverage. The [[Underwriter]] is the gatekeeper.

This is the core function. This is how The Pool defends itself against [[Adverse Selection]]. The underwriter's job is to say "yes" or "no," and if "yes," for how much and under what conditions. They use [[Underwriting Guidelines]], [[Predictive Modeling]], and their own judgment to decide if your particular risk is a good fit for their Pool. They are the ones who try to make sure the rock climbers don't get in at the office-worker price. The entire financial stability of The Pool rests on good underwriting.
