---
title: Balance Sheet
---

The [[Balance Sheet]] is the snapshot of how much trouble the insurance company is in. It's based on the beautiful equation Assets = Liabilities + Policyholders' Surplus. The [[Assets]] are what they own, the [[Liabilities]] are mostly their best guess at future claims ([[Loss Reserves]]), and the [[Policyholders Surplus]] is what's left over—the cushion that proves they can probably pay you if your house burns down.
