---
title: Solvency
---
Solvency is an insurance company's ability to pay its debts and meet its financial obligations, especially all those future claims. It's the opposite of insolvency, which is when [[The Pool]] is empty.

This is the single most important thing. All of [[Insurance Regulation]] is fundamentally about ensuring solvency. It doesn't matter how cheap the premiums are if the company can't pay the claim when your house burns down. Regulators force companies to hold a certain amount of [[Policyholders Surplus]], to invest in safe [[Assets]], and to use conservative accounting. It's all to make sure that when you finally need to take money out of The Pool, the money is actually there.
