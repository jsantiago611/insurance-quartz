---
title: Brokerage
---

[[Brokerage]] is the commission paid to a reinsurance intermediary for placing business with a [[Reinsurance|reinsurer]].

Okay, so you have an insurer, and they have their own [[The Pool]], but it's too big for them, so they need to offload some of it. They go to a broker. The broker shops it around to other, bigger Pools—the reinsurers. When a deal is made, the reinsurer pays the broker a fee. This fee is the brokerage. It's a finder's fee. It's a meta-cost for connecting one pool of capital to another. The whole system is just layers of people connecting pools and taking a cut.
