---
title: Brokerage
---

[[Brokerage]] is the commission paid to a reinsurance intermediary. It's the finder's fee for connecting an insurer with a [[Reinsurance|reinsurer]]. It's the same as any other brokerage business: you introduce two parties who want to do a deal, and you take a cut. The fact that the deal is about one pool of money insuring another pool of money just makes it more meta.
