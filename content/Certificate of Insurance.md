---
title: Certificate of Insurance
---
A [[Certificate of Insurance]] is a document that proves you have a policy. It's a snapshot that says, "Yep, this person is in [[The Pool]]."

This is the "show your work" of the insurance world. You don't carry around your 100-page policy. When a landlord or a general contractor wants proof you're covered, you show them this. It's the insurance equivalent of a concert ticket—it doesn't get you into The Pool, but it proves you paid to be there. It's a piece of paper that lets other people stop worrying about your risk.
