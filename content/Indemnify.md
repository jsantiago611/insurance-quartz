---
title: Indemnify
---
To [[Indemnify]] someone is to restore them to the same financial position they were in before a loss occurred. It's the core promise of insurance.

This is the whole point. This is the "making whole." 

- The principle of [[Principle of Indemnity|Indemnity]] is why you can't profit from an insurance claim. 
- It's why you get [[Actual Cash Value]] instead of [[Replacement Cost]] unless you pay extra. 
- It's why there's [[Subrogation]]—if The Pool pays you, they get your right to sue the person who caused the loss. 

The goal is to put you back to where you were, not to make you better off. The Pool is not a lottery; it's a safety net.
