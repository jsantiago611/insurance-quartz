---
title: Insured
---
The Insured is the person or entity covered by the insurance policy. They are the one who is allowed into [[The Pool]].

This seems like the simplest concept, but it gets tricky fast. On a personal auto policy, who is the insured? Just the person named on the declarations page? Or also their resident relatives? What about someone borrowing the car with permission? The definition of "insured" is everything. It determines who has the right to a defense, who gets the claim payment, who is protected. It's not always just the person who pays the bill. You have to read the policy. It's the answer to the question, "For whom does this Pool exist?"
